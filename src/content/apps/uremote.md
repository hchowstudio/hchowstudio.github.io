The free TV remote your iPhone deserves. URemote controls Roku, Samsung, LG and Google TV over Wi-Fi with a clean, native design and zero ads.

Lost the remote? Tired of cluttered remote apps full of pop-ups? URemote finds your smart TV on your network, pairs in seconds and feels right at home on iOS: calm, fast and dark-first.

### Works With

- Roku TVs and streaming players (including TCL, Hisense and onn Roku TVs)
- Samsung Smart TVs (Tizen)
- LG Smart TVs (webOS)
- Google TV and Android TV, including Chromecast with Google TV

### Your Whole Remote, On Your Phone

- Full button remote: power, D-pad, OK, back, home, volume, mute, channels and playback
- Press and hold to repeat, for fast volume changes and quick scrolling
- Subtle haptics so every press feels real
- Reconnects automatically when you open the app

### Touchpad & Keyboard

- Swipe and tap on a large touchpad to move through menus
- Type searches, emails and passwords with your iPhone keyboard instead of pecking at letters one by one

### Apps At A Tap

- See the apps installed on your TV and launch any of them instantly

### Cast To Your Tv

- Send photos from your library to the big screen, with slideshows
- With Premium, cast videos and video links too, and control them from the Lock Screen

### Private By Design

- No ads, ever
- No account and no sign-up
- Your TV pairing details stay on your device

### DESIGNED FOR iPHONE

- Built with native iOS technology, not a web wrapper
- A dark "Midnight & Ember" look that is easy on the eyes in a dark room
- Works on iPad too

### How It Works

1. Connect your iPhone and TV to the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Open URemote and choose your TV, or add it by IP address.
3. Confirm the pairing prompt on your TV if one appears.
4. Start controlling.

### Requirements

- A Roku, Samsung, LG or Google/Android TV connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone
- URemote works over Wi-Fi only. iPhone has no infrared (IR) blaster, so older TVs that are not connected to your network are not supported
- Some features, such as turning the TV on, depend on your TV model and its network settings
