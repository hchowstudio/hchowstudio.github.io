Turn any paper into a clean, searchable PDF in seconds. ScanDoc finds the edges, flattens the page and sharpens the text — and everything stays on your iPhone.

### Scan Anything

- Document scanner with automatic edge detection and auto-capture
- Batch mode: scan multi-page documents in one go
- ID card & passport mode — both sides on one page
- Receipts, contracts, notes, whiteboards, books
- QR code scanner with history

### Make It Perfect

- Smart crop, rotate and perspective correction
- Filters: Magic Color, Black & White, Grayscale, Lighten
- Brightness and contrast controls

### Edit, Sign & Fill

- Draw, scan or import your signature — add stamps like Approved or Paid
- Edit PDFs: add text in your choice of font and color, shapes, arrows, highlights, images and drawings
- Fill in forms, tick boxes and add notes

### Extract, Translate & Ask Ai

- OCR: copy and search the text in any scan
- Translate whole pages — offline, on-device
- Ask AI: summarize a document, ask questions, pull out key dates and amounts (requires Apple Intelligence)

### Convert & Share

- Photo, JPG, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files to PDF
- Export as PDF, JPG, Word or TXT
- Reorder, rotate, delete, extract and insert pages
- Merge and split PDFs, compress large files for email
- Share to Mail, Files, Messages, or print

### Organize & Protect

- Folders, search and sorting
- Lock folders with Face ID and protect PDFs with a password
- Private by design: your scans are processed and stored only on your iPhone
