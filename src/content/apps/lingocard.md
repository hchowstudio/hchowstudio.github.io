Learn with Flashcards of Sentences and Phrases

LingoCard is your go-to app for mastering languages through practical sentence and phrase flashcards. Whether you’re a beginner or advancing your fluency, LingoCard is designed to accelerate your language learning with customizable collections and interactive practice features.

Key Features:

- Built-in Essential Phrase Collections

Access a wide variety of essential phrase collections, including top conversation phrases, travel phrases, workplace phrase and more! LingoCard provides curated content to help you quickly master key sentences for real-world situations.

- Create Unlimited Custom Collections with Text-to-Speech Support

Personalize your language learning by building your own collections of sentences and phrases. Text-to-speech for your any sentence you add, and all collections are stored securely on your device.

- Customizable Flashcards

Adjust font styles, text colors, and background themes to make your learning environment as engaging and personalized as possible.

- Interactive Audio Practice

Listen, repeat, and improve your pronunciation with audio card for every sentence. Practice speaking confidently at your own pace.

- **Dictation Mode:** Sharpen Memory and Writing Skills

Strengthen both memory and writing skills through dictation exercises. Listen to sentences and type or speak the responses, with instant feedback to track your progress and enhance retention.

- Practice Anytime, Anywhere

Take LingoCard with you and practice at your convenience. Whether you’re at home or on the move, LingoCard fits into your schedule, helping you improve consistently.

Why LingoCard?

LingoCard focuses on practical, real-world phrases to help you communicate effectively. By learning through sentence flashcards, you’ll build confidence and memory retention for fluid conversations. Whether you’re preparing for a trip, an interview, or just enhancing your language skills, LingoCard is the perfect tool to help you succeed.
