Master English Pronunciation Effortlessly with Pronounce English: IPA Coach

Tired of mumbling through conversations or struggling with tricky English sounds? Pronounce English: IPA Coach is your personal guide to crystal-clear pronunciation. Featuring interactive IPA charts, bite-sized pronunciation lessons, you'll build confidence in speaking, listening, and shadowing - like a native speaker - in just minutes a day. Whether you’re learning English as a second language or looking to refine your accent, YouSpeak provides the tools you need to speak confidently and clearly.

Features:

- **Phonemic Chart:** Explore a comprehensive IPA chart with detailed examples and audio guides for each phonetic symbol. Perfect for learners and linguists alike!
- **Interactive Exercises:** Practice speaking with tailored exercises that cover common English sounds.
- **Record your practice:** self-assess, learn, and improve daily.
- **Bookmark Favorites:** Save your favorite exercises and phonetic symbols for quick access and continuous practice.
- **Customizable Learning:** Adjust the text size, theme, and audio settings to create a personalized learning environment that suits your needs.

Why Choose YouSpeak?

- **Interactive IPA Chart:** Visualize all 44 English sounds with animated mouth positions and phonetic symbols. Tap to hear examples in American or British accents, perfect for beginners decoding the International Phonetic Alphabet.
- **Pronunciation Lessons:** Structured modules cover vowels, consonants, stress patterns, and intonation. Learn rules like silent letters or diphthongs through fun, guided audio drills.
- **Listening Practice with Transcripts:** Immerse in real dialogues and podcasts. Pause, replay, and follow along with full, tappable transcripts to sharpen comprehension and ear training.
- **Speaking Practice with Voice Recording:** Record yourself anytime, anywhere. Playback with waveform visuals to self-assess rhythm and clarity—great for offline travel sessions.
- **Advanced Shadowing Technique:** Mimic native speakers in AI-powered exercises. Our speech-to-text engine transcribes your voice instantly, highlighting matches/mismatches against original transcripts for targeted feedback. Adjust speeds from slow-motion to natural for progressive mastery.
