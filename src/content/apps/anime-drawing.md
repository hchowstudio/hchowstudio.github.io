Point your phone. Trace the outline. Draw like you've been practicing for years.

- **Anime Drawing:** AR Tracing turns your phone into a live drawing guide — project any reference onto real paper using AR camera overlay, let your screen glow like a lightbox beneath the page, or trace directly on screen. Three modes. One app.

### Anime & Manga Library

Start with the details that define anime art:
- Eyes: shonen bold, shojo sparkly, chibi huge, heterochromia, star pupils
- Face proportions:, 3/4 angle, side profile, looking up and down
- Hair: spiky, flowing, twin tails, ahoge, undercut, silver, bob
- Hands: fist, open palm, pointing, gripping, elegant
- Poses: standing, action, sitting, jumping, battle stance
- Expressions: happy, angry, surprised, blushing, determined
- Chibi characters: kawaii, simple to trace, beginner-perfect
- Manga style: screentone lines, classic B&W, webtoon, shojo softness

### Draw Yourself In Anime

Use the AR camera to trace your own face and redraw it in anime style. The most-shared drawing trend on social media and your camera makes it easier than ever.

### Three Tracing Modes

- AR Camera live overlay on paper, real time
- **Lightbox:** screen glows beneath your page like a pro lightbox
- **On-Screen:** trace directly on your display with finger or stylus

### Load Any Image

Import from your photo library, take a new photo. Trace your pet, your partner, your tattoo design, your OC anything.

Whether you're tracing your first anime eye or building an original character sheet, this is the fastest way to improve your drawing accuracy, learn proportions, and make art you're proud of.
