QRWallet is the fast, ad-free QR code scanner and creator that keeps all your codes in one place — on your iPhone, synced with iCloud, and one tap away on your Home Screen.

Scan any QR code or barcode in a second. Create beautiful custom codes for your Wi-Fi, business card, website or social profile. Then save them to your wallet so you never dig through screenshots again.

### No Ads. Ever.

QRWallet was rebuilt from the ground up in native SwiftUI. No banners, no pop-ups, no tracking — just a clean, fast app that feels right at home on iOS.

### Scan Instantly

- Point your camera at any QR code or barcode
- Scan from photos and screenshots in your library
- Open links, join Wi-Fi, save contacts and more in one tap
- Every scan is saved to your history automatically

### Create Custom Qr Codes

- Website links and social profiles
- Wi-Fi networks — let guests connect without typing a password
- Contact cards (vCard) for networking
- Email, phone, SMS, text and more
- Customize colors, shapes and frames, and add your logo
- Export and share as a high-quality image

### Your Qr Wallet

- Keep every scanned and created code organized in one place
- Pin the codes you use most: tickets, passes, menus, membership and loyalty cards, payment codes
- Find any code in seconds with search

### Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets

Show your favorite QR code right on your Home Screen. At the gym, the office, a concert gate or the checkout counter, your code is ready before you even open the app.

### Icloud Sync & Privacy

Your codes are stored on your device and synced privately through your own iCloud account. Create a code on your iPhone and it's waiting on your iPad. No sign-up, no account, no servers of ours.
