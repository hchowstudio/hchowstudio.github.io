The interval timer that keeps you coming back. Tabata, HIIT, EMOM, AMRAP and For Time, all free, with no ads and no tracking.

Build any workout in seconds, train with clear voice cues, and keep your streak going with a progress grid that fills up every day you show up.

### Every Timer You Need

- Tabata, Intervals, EMOM, AMRAP, For Time, Countdown, Stopwatch
- Mix: stack blocks with their own timing per exercise
- Tap to count AMRAP rounds, with split times
- Runs in the background, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island

### Coaching That Talks To You

- Voice announces each phase, the next exercise and time left
- 3-2-1 countdowns, halfway cues, haptics and screen flash
- Your music keeps playing and lowers automatically for cues

### Build The Habit

- Streaks, weekly goals and a year-long progress grid
- Achievements and shareable workout cards
- Effort rating and notes after every session

### Train Smarter

- 30-day programs for fat burn, strength and consistency
- Exercise library with demos and form tips
- Warm-up and cool-down stretches matched to the muscles you trained
- Friend challenges: send a link, compare rounds, no account needed

### Apple Ecosystem

- Apple Watch app with heart rate
- Saves to Apple Health
- Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, iCloud sync

### Private By Design

No ads. No tracking. No sign-up.

### Roundly Pro

Every timer stays free. Pro adds the Apple Watch app, programs, stats and records, widgets and iCloud sync. Choose monthly, yearly (with free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage them in your Apple ID settings.
